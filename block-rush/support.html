App Store name: Block Rush Online

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: yousef.develop@icloud.com. Please include your display name and your iPhone model. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

  • Memorize: a block shape appears on every island for a few seconds.
  • Rebuild: it disappears. Rebuild it on your island by tapping the floor or the side of a block.
  • Survive: the lowest score is hit by a shooting star. The last island standing wins.

Frequently asked questions

How many free games do I get?
2 free games every day. They reset at midnight in your time zone.

How do I play with friends?
Tap Private, create a room and share the 4-letter code. Friends join with that code.

Why can't an online match start?
Online rooms need at least 5 players (8-player rooms) or 9 players (12-player rooms).

I bought something but don't see it.
Open the store (the + button) and tap Restore Purchases. If it's still missing, email us.

How do I cancel Unlimited?
In your iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Block Rush Online.

How do I delete my data?
Email us with your display name. See the Privacy Policy.
